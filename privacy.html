Metas Deck privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Metas Deck is a private tool that Meta uses to publish Meta's own videos to the Metas Projects YouTube channel and to reply to comments on that channel. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses. Only the Metas Projects YouTube channel, through the YouTube Data API, with Meta's permission: uploading videos, reading comments on the channel's videos, posting replies, and reading view counts.

What it stores. The sign-in token is stored encrypted on Meta's own computer. Comment text and view counts are kept there to plan replies and posts. Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Metas Deck's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: metasprojects@gmail.com
